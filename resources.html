Career Guidance & Safety Guidelines

Essential tips to build your professional application and protect yourself online.

🛡️ Identifying Recruitment & Employment Scams

  • Never Pay Fees: Legitimate companies or SETA programs will never ask for application fees, uniform payments, or medical clearance money via Money Market or WhatsApp.
  • Check Sender Domains: Official communication comes from corporate domain emails (e.g., name@company.co.za), not free addresses (@gmail.com).
  • Unrealistic Offers: High salaries for low requirements are common warning signs.

📄 Writing an Effective CV

Keep your resume concise (1-2 pages maximum). Save your document in PDF format to ensure consistent layout rendering across recruiters' phones and PCs.

✉️ Crafting a Cover Letter

Tailor your cover letter to each position. Highlight your relevant project work, skills, and exact qualifications rather than sending a generic copy.

View Cover Letter Sample

🎯 Interview Preparation

Prepare to answer situational questions using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Practice articulating your problem-solving process out loud.
